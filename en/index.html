Innovative Solutions for Advanced Plastic Recycling

Transforming Plastic Waste into High-Value Resources
#

Aceretech is dedicated to advancing the plastic recycling industry through engineering excellence and sustainable innovation. Our mission is to empower the global recycling community with technology that converts environmental challenges into economic opportunities.

Comprehensive Recycling Systems
#

Aceretech offers a range of high-performance recycling solutions tailored to various plastic waste streams:

  • PET Recycling Line: Advanced turnkey systems convert post-consumer PET bottles into high-quality flakes or food-grade pellets. Integrated technologies such as SSP and Liquid State Polymerization (LSP) enhance intrinsic viscosity and purity, meeting global food safety standards and supporting recyclers in achieving carbon reduction goals. Learn more

  • Plastic Recycling Pelletizing Line: Designed for PE, PP, PET, films, and rigid plastics, these systems integrate compaction, plasticizing, filtration, and pelletizing. The result is stable operation, consistent melt flow, and moisture levels below 0.5%. Energy-efficient and flexible, these lines support reliable production of high-density recycled pellets. Learn more

  • Integrated Washing and Pelletizing Solution: Combining cleaning and granulation, this system processes post-consumer and industrial waste, including films, rigid plastics, and regrind materials. It improves material purity, ensures consistent pellet quality, and enhances energy efficiency. Learn more

  • Crushers & Shredders: Robust machines for efficient size reduction of films, rigid plastics, and bulky waste. These systems ensure uniform output and stable operation, supporting downstream processing and enhancing material handling efficiency. Learn more

Key Advantages for Recycling Professionals
#

  • Global Experience: With over 1,350 installations across 93 countries, Aceretech brings cross-regional expertise to diverse waste streams and regulatory environments.
  • Technical Excellence: Our systems consistently achieve final moisture levels below 0.5% and up to 88% energy efficiency compared to traditional methods.
  • Quality Assurance: Specialized technologies, such as SSP for food-grade PET and dual-vacuum degassing, ensure maximum purity and premium output quality.
  • Full Lifecycle Support: From factory layout and custom engineering to overseas installation and rapid after-sales service, Aceretech is committed to long-term partnership in the circular economy.
