Product Portfolio
#

ACERETECH offers a comprehensive range of products designed to meet diverse plastic recycling needs:

  • PET Recycling Line: Efficient solutions for PET bottle recycling, including food-grade bottle-to-bottle systems.
  • Recycling Pelletizing System: Advanced pelletizing lines for various plastic materials.
  • Crusher & Shredder: Equipment for shredding and crushing waste plastics.
  • Washing And Pelletizing Turn-key Solution: Integrated systems for washing and pelletizing.
  • Auxiliary Machine For Plastic Recycling: Supporting machinery for enhanced recycling efficiency.
